07_Codex Ad Infinitum

The Library is a vast repository of information. IN our accelerating image culture knowledge is now predominantly spread through images not facts, words, or real experiences. Here, we are experiencing Beptrost a previously unknown words I discovered in the library, and speculated on its meaning using chat gpt. The library can help us re-wild language, but most importantly we must be rigorous, structured, and intentional about the way we move about in it. Witrhout a framework for image borrowing there is great danger in the misuse of images and the information that flows upon them.
