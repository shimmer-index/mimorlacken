01_The AI Bureau

IS YOUR CITY IN A POST-INDUSTRIAL SLUMBER?
IS YOUR CITY CHOKED BY STAGNANT MODERNISM?
DOES YOUR CITY LACK IDENTITY?
THEN YOUR CITY NEEDS AN AI BUREAU!

With the rise of AI slopaganda and increasing urbanisation, the cityscape is rapidly changing. The city is on the precipice of opportunity and change. Coventry, struggling in its post-industrial slumber is the first to fully embrace the new city – the AI integrated city. Exploring the new realm of ring road architecture, a site on the East side of the inner city ringroad – now pedestrianised – will be the location for the world’s first AI Bureau.
